# Client brief

Fill this from the conversation. Write the client's own words where possible, and never invent facts.
Write "unknown" for any fact the user has not given. Do not guess, and do not fill gaps with benchmarks.
Ask the user once, in a single message, for every missing (critical) field; whatever is still missing after that stays "unknown" and becomes an assumption.

## Client and offer (critical)
_Company name, what it sells, price point or deal size, and the specific product, service or offer this work is about._

## Work type (critical)
_Exactly one of: campaign | audit | pitch | creative | custom: <what>. For custom, name the work, for example "custom: measurement roadmap"._

## Business goal (critical)
_The business outcome the work serves (pipeline, revenue, profitable growth, launch, awareness), in the client's words, with any figure they gave._

## Objective and KPIs (critical)
_The primary KPI with target, unit and period (for example 400 MQLs per month at or under 150 USD CPL), secondary KPIs, and how each conversion is defined and where it is recorded._

## Budget (critical)
_Amount, currency and period (per month, per flight or total), and whether agency fees and production are included. If none was given, write "no budget given" and seats will plan in percentages._

## Timeline (critical)
_Today's date, launch date, flight end, and key dates (holidays, sales events, review lead times, approval deadlines), all as YYYY-MM-DD._

## Geography and language (critical)
_Markets (countries or regions), languages for ads and pages, and any split between markets._

## Audience
_Who the work must reach: segments, roles or demographics, needs, objections, and anything known about how they buy._

## Channels in scope and out of scope
_Channels and workstreams the client wants, those they have ruled out, and any they have tried before, with the result._

## Current state
_Ad accounts and access; tracking status (conversion events defined? Enhanced Conversions? CAPI? consent mode and CMP? CRM or offline import?); CRM or store platform; past performance with date ranges._

## Assets and resources
_Brand guidelines, existing creative, landing pages and CMS, developer capacity (days per week), production capacity and budget._

## Competitors
_Named competitors and alternatives, in the client's words, with anything known about their positioning or activity._

## Mandatories and constraints
_Legal and regulatory rules, regulated category, claims review and its lead time, brand rules, platform restrictions, must-use and must-avoid._

## Approvals and stakeholders
_Who signs off, who is the day-to-day contact, who else must be consulted, and how long approvals take._

## Data files and links
_Absolute paths to exports and screenshots, and URLs for the orchestrator to snapshot into the run's inputs folder; for each, the date range, currency and attribution setting if known._

## Deliverable format
_What the client expects to receive: document type, length, structure, file format, and who will read it._

## Anything else
_Anything the user said that does not fit above: history, internal politics, preferences, worries._
